✦ BDS Course

BDS Course Details

The field of dentistry plays a crucial role in modern healthcare by promoting oral health and treating dental conditions through advanced clinical techniques and specialized care. It is a rapidly growing profession that blends medical science, precision, and patient care, offering students the opportunity to build a rewarding career in the healthcare sector. Across Karnataka, several reputed institutions offer professional dental education that provide the requisite clinical exposure needed for aspiring dentists that includes the necessary skills along with the right infrastructure.

Bachelor of Dental Surgery (BDS) is the foundational UG programme that equips students with in-depth theoretical knowledge and practical training in oral healthcare, diagnosis, treatment procedures, and patient management. For students planning to pursue BDS after qualifying NEET, having a clear understanding of the course, eligibility criteria, and admission process is essential to making an informed decision.

Course Name Bachelor of Dental Surgery (BDS)
Course Level Undergraduate (UG)
Duration 5 Years (4 Years Academia + 1 Year Internship)
Entrance Exam NEET-UG
BDS Eligibility Criteria
  • Passed 10+2 / 2nd PU with English and minimum 50% aggregate in PCB
  • Qualified NEET-UG entrance exam
  • Must be 17 years of age as of December 31st in the year of admission

✦ Admission Process

BDS Admissions

Securing BDS admission in India can be a task on its own. It has two stages, with the first stage being qualifying for the NEET-UG exam. Once the candidates have cleared the exam, then they will be eligible for the second stage, i.e., the counselling process. The BDS counselling process takes place at two levels: national and state.

The All-India (national) level counselling is conducted by the Medical Counselling Committee (MCC), and the state-level counselling is managed by respective state bodies. In the case of Karnataka, the counselling process is governed by the KEA. After securing a seat, candidates must report to the allotted dental college and submit the required documents listed below to complete the BDS admission process.

✦ FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

What is BDS course duration?
The BDS course duration is 5 years. This undergraduate dental programme includes 4 years of academic study followed by a 1-year compulsory rotatory internship with clinical training across various dental specializations.
What is the BDS course eligibility criteria?
Candidates must have completed 10+2 / 2nd PUC / equivalent examination with Physics, Chemistry, Biology, and English as mandatory subjects. They must secure the minimum qualifying marks prescribed for their category and must qualify NEET-UG for admission.
Can I pursue BDS without NEET?
No, admission to BDS colleges in India requires candidates to qualify NEET-UG, as it is the mandatory entrance examination for dental admissions.
What subjects are included in the BDS syllabus?
The BDS syllabus covers subjects such as Human Anatomy, Physiology, Dental Anatomy, Pathology, Pharmacology, Oral Medicine, Orthodontics, Periodontology, Prosthodontics, Oral Surgery, and Public Health Dentistry, along with practical clinical training.
What are the higher study options after BDS?
After BDS, students can pursue MDS (Master of Dental Surgery) in specialized branches such as Orthodontics, Oral Surgery, Prosthodontics, Periodontology, Endodontics, and Paediatric Dentistry.
