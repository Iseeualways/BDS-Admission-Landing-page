✦ BDS Course Details

BDS Course Details

The Bachelor of Dental Surgery (BDS) undergraduate course integrates theoretical knowledge of diagnosis with practical skills necessary to treat and prevent oral ailments. The curriculum prepares students to learn dental procedures, understand usage of dental equipment, develop work ethics, and follow legal aspects of the dental world.

This shapes them into healthcare professionals who can make a real difference in the world of dentistry.

Course Name Bachelor of Dental Surgery (BDS)
Course Level Undergraduate (UG)
Duration 5 Years (4 Years Academia + 1 Year Internship)
Entrance Exam NEET-UG
BDS Eligibility Criteria
  • Passed 10+2 / 2nd PU with English and min. 50% aggregate in PCB
  • Qualified NEET-UG entrance exam
  • Must be 17 years as of December 31st in the year of admission
Regulatory Body Dental Council of India (DCI)

✦ Admission Process

BDS Admissions

Securing BDS admission in India has two stages. The first is qualifying the NEET-UG exam. Candidates who cleared the exam are eligible for the second stage — the counselling process.

The BDS counselling process takes place at two levels: national and state. The All-India level counselling is conducted by the Medical Counselling Committee (MCC), and state-level counselling is managed by respective state bodies.

In the case of Karnataka, the counselling process is governed by the KEA.

✦ FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Is there any entrance exam for BDS admission in Karnataka?
Yes, candidates have to compulsorily qualify the National Eligibility-cum-Entrance Test - Undergraduate (NEET-UG) exam to be eligible for BDS admission in Karnataka / BDS admission in Bangalore.
What is the BDS Eligibility Criteria?
The BDS course eligibility criteria are as follows: students must have completed their 10+2 / 2nd PUC with a minimum aggregate of 50% marks in PCB and English, along with clearing the NEET-UG entrance exam.
What is the BDS course duration?
The BDS course duration is 5 years, which includes 4 years of academic study followed by 1 year of compulsory internship.
How much is the BDS course fees in Karnataka?
In Karnataka, as in every other state across India, the BDS fee structure in private colleges will vary from that in government institutes. The Bachelor of Dental Surgery course fees depend on the type of seat a candidate is eligible for, i.e., Merit or Management.
What are some of the best private dental colleges in Bangalore?
Faculty of Dental Sciences at Ramaiah University of Applied Sciences (RUAS), BGS Global Institute of Dental Sciences, Vokkaligara Sangha Dental College & Hospital (KIMS Campus), and Vydehi Institute of Dental Sciences & Research Centre are some of the best private dental colleges in Bangalore.
